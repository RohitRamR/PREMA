## Brand & Style
This design system powers an India-first, compatibility-centered relationship experience. It discards casino gamification, aggressive swipe patterns, and neon gradients in favor of an Apple-level clarity, editorial poise, and thoughtful spatial rhythm. The target audience comprises intentional urban singles looking for deep alignment in core values, life vision, family dynamics, and lifestyle.

The emotional signature is grounded, dignified, and emotionally safe. The aesthetic bridges high-end architectural editorial layout with tactile mobile precision: warm off-white natural canvas, crisp card surfaces, deep navy typographic hierarchy, and gentle sky/powder blue accents that invite breath and patience. Every micro-interaction is tuned to evoke respect and intentionality rather than urgency.

## Colors
The palette balances warm organic neutrality with crisp structural contrast:

- **Primary (`#172B3A`)**: Deep Navy. Serves as the primary typographic anchor, high-intent action triggers (primary buttons, active states), and solid iconography.
- **Secondary (`#A9D8F5`)**: Soft Sky Blue. Reserved for compatibility scores, verification badges, secondary action highlights, and focused interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#DCEFFA`)**: Powder Blue. Used for quiet surface fills, inactive pill badges, prompt reply backplates, and subtle ambient highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F7F2`)**: Warm Off-White. The root page canvas that replaces harsh sterile digital whites with editorial warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure White. Dedicated solely to elevated profile cards, sheets, and input fields.
- **Muted Typography (`#5C6D79`)**: Slate Navy. Provides WCAG AA-compliant contrast for metadata, timestamps, and secondary field labels.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all tiers for its welcoming geometric structure, balanced proportions, and clean letter terminals.

- **Display & Headline Levels**: Employ tight letter spacing (-0.03em to -0.015em) and intentional optical weight to evoke premium publication qualities.
- **Body Levels**: Engineered with generous leading (1.5x) to facilitate fluid reading of personal essays, compatibility values, and life philosophy prompts.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Medium to bold tracking ensures metadata chips, verification stamps, and compatibility percentages remain legible at glanceable mobile scales.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model prioritizes single-column mobile-first flow anchored by safe-area padding and comfortable thumb reach zones:

- **Mobile Viewport (375px - 430px)**: 16px to 20px horizontal page margins. Content cards float 16px off edges or present full-bleed top media with curved 24px bottom sheet enclosures.
- **Tablet / Large Screens (600px+)**: Content constrains strictly to a centered 480px max-width vessel to preserve tactile intimate vertical orientation.
- **Spacing Cadence**: Built upon a strict 4px/8px baseline. Content elements inside profile prompt units utilize 12px and 16px gaps, while major narrative sections enforce 24px to 32px breathing room to avoid information fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through physical softness, natural light diffusion, and tonal layering rather than hard artificial drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Warm off-white `#F8F7F2` flat background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Containers)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Thumb Controls & Overlays)**: Deep Navy or Glass buttons elevated with `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Verification Sheets)**: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.18)`.
- **Glass Surfaces**: Translucent media overlays utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` at 85% opacity mixed with subtle 1px border stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.

## Shapes
The design uses an organic, tactile aesthetic calibrated specifically for touch:

- **Profile & Narrative Cards**: 24px corner radius (`rounded-2xl`), creating an inviting, book-like presence.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) for high ergonomics and tap affordance.
- **Input Fields & Prompt Containers**: 20px corner radius with seamless internal nesting.
- **Sub-elements & Avatars**: Circular 1:1 ratios for profile thumbs and 16px to 20px for inline media snippets.

## Components

### Primary & Action Buttons
- **Primary**: Pill-shaped, Deep Navy `#172B3A` background, Pure White typography (`label-lg`), minimum touch target height of 54px. Active state transitions with scale reduction to `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Soft Pill**: Powder Blue `#DCEFFA` background with Deep Navy `#172B3A` text.
- **Floating Decision Triggers**: Twin circular or soft pill buttons (Pass / Connect) suspended above photo canvas with frosted backdrop glass or high-contrast solid fills.

### Chips & Compatibility Badges
- **Value & Trait Chips**: Powder Blue `#DCEFFA` background with 1px border `rgba(169, 216, 245, 0.5)`. Text in `label-md` `#172B3A`.
- **Alignment Match Badge**: Pill indicator featuring Soft Sky Blue `#A9D8F5` filled dot, bold percentage, and category label (e.g., "94% Alignment • Core Values").

### Profile Cards & Content Modules
- **Hero Imagery**: 24px rounded corners, aspect ratio 4:5 or 3:4, subtle inner shadow vignette at the base to ensure metadata readability.
- **Narrative Prompts**: Clean white cards with `headline-sm` questions in Deep Navy and answer copy in `body-lg`. Generous internal padding (20px).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text & Response Fields**: Off-white to white background with 1.5px subtle border `rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.1)`. Focus state shifts border cleanly to Deep Navy `#172B3A` with no harsh glow rings.
- **Segmented Controls**: Inset Powder Blue `#DCEFFA` pill track with Pure White sliding active segment indicator and smooth 200ms cubic-bezier transition.

### Lists & Value Matrices
- **Compatibility Lists**: 16px vertical spacing with light divider line `#EBE9E1`. Iconographic markers in Deep Navy with Soft Sky Blue fill circles.
